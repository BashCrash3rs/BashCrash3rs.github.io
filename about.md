We are **BashCrash3rs**, a CTF team based in Houston, Texas. This is our corner of the interwebz: a home for our CTF writeups, the paths that worked, and the things we learned getting there.

## Curiosity is the common thread.

Our interests span web exploitation, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, OSINT, and steganography. When we’re not competing in a CTF, we’re usually auditing code, configuring networks, and converting caffeine into clean syntax.
